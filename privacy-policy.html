1 Data We Collect

ToolBoxSuite is designed to collect as little data as possible. Here is what we may collect:

  • Basic anonymous usage analytics (pages visited, tool usage counts) — no personal identifiers
  • Browser type and device type for compatibility improvements
  • General geographic region (country level) for analytics

We do not collect your name, email address, IP address, or any personal information unless you contact us directly.

2 How Your Files Are Processed

This is the most important section. Every tool on ToolBoxSuite runs entirely inside your browser using WebAssembly and on-device AI technologies.

  • Your files are never uploaded to our servers
  • Your files are never transmitted over the internet
  • Your files are never stored anywhere outside your own device
  • Processing happens locally on your CPU/GPU
  • When you close or refresh the page, all file data is cleared from memory

This architecture means we are physically incapable of accessing the files you process on ToolBoxSuite.

3 Cookies & Analytics

We may use minimal, privacy-respecting analytics to understand how our tools are used so we can improve them. Any analytics we use:

  • Do not track you across websites
  • Do not build personal profiles
  • Do not sell data to advertisers

We do not use advertising cookies or retargeting pixels of any kind.

4 Third-Party Services

ToolBoxSuite uses the following third-party services to function:

  • Google Fonts — for typography (fonts are loaded from Google's CDN)
  • Web hosting / CDN provider — for serving the website files

These services have their own privacy policies and may log standard web request data (IP addresses, user agents) as part of normal CDN/hosting operation. We have no control over this data.

5 Children's Privacy

ToolBoxSuite does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Our tools are general-purpose productivity tools suitable for all ages. Since we collect no personal data, there is no special risk to children using our tools.

6 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of ToolBoxSuite after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
