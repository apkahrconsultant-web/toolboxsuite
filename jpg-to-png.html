How to Use JPG to PNG Converter

Upload any JPEG, JPG, WebP, or BMP image. Our tool re-encodes it as a lossless PNG file, preserving every detail of the original. PNG format supports transparency and lossless compression — preferred for logos, icons, screenshots, and digital art.

Why Privacy Matters

Image files contain embedded metadata — EXIF data with GPS coordinates, camera settings, timestamps. ToolBoxSuite converts your image format using the browser's built-in Canvas API.
