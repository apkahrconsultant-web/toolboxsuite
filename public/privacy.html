Last updated: June 2025

🔒 The short version: ToolBoxSuite processes all your files locally in your browser. We never upload, store, or transmit your documents or images to any server. We do not sell your personal data.

ToolBoxSuite ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website toolboxsuite.in. We provide free, browser-based tools for image and document processing. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at tooboxsuite@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

We collect as little data as possible to operate the website.

Files you process: All image and document processing happens entirely within your browser using WebAssembly and on-device AI. Your files are never uploaded to our servers. We have no technical ability to access the files you process.

Usage data: We may collect anonymised usage data such as which pages you visit, how long you stay, and which tools you use. This data does not identify you personally and is used only to improve the website.

Contact form data: If you contact us, we collect the name and email address you provide solely to respond to your enquiry.

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate and improve the website. These include:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic website functionality.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

4. Third-party services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics — website usage analytics. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense — display advertising. Google Privacy Policy

We use the data we collect to:

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

6. Data retention

Since we do not receive or store your files, there is no file data to retain or delete. Contact form data is retained only as long as necessary to resolve your enquiry. Analytics data is retained in anonymised form as per Google Analytics' standard retention policies.
