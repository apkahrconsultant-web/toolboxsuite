What we do

ToolBoxSuite is a collection of free online tools that help you remove image backgrounds, convert documents, compress images, merge PDFs, and more — all without uploading your files to any server.

Every tool runs entirely inside your browser using WebAssembly and on-device AI. Your files never leave your device, no account is required, and there are no hidden fees.

🔒
Privacy first

All processing happens locally. Zero bytes are uploaded to any server, ever.

⚡
Instant results

No upload queues or server delays. Your device does the work in seconds.

🆓
Free forever

No watermarks, no daily limits, no subscriptions. All tools are completely free.

📱
Works everywhere

Fully optimised for mobile, tablet, and desktop on any modern browser.
