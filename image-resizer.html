How to Use Image Resizer

Upload a PNG, JPG, or WebP image. The tool displays its current dimensions with editable width and height fields. Toggle the aspect ratio lock to maintain proportions automatically. Enter your target dimensions and click Resize & Download.

Why Privacy Matters

Resizing images through a cloud service means your original high-resolution files are uploaded and potentially cached on remote servers. ToolBoxSuite's resizer uses the browser's native Canvas API to scale your image on-device.
