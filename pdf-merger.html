How to Use PDF Merger

Select two or more PDF files by clicking the upload area or dragging them in. Files appear in a numbered list showing names and sizes. Click "Merge & Download" and the tool combines all pages in order into one unified document.

Why Privacy Matters

When merging PDFs you're often combining confidential documents — employment records, legal briefs, medical forms. ToolBoxSuite uses the pdf-lib library to merge documents entirely in your browser's memory. Each PDF is read as a byte array, pages are copied programmatically, and the final file is assembled without any server interaction.
